# Decisions

Record significant accepted architecture and project-wide design decisions here. Once accepted, an ADR is a historical record, not a progress report. Use the next number after the highest ADR; if replacing a decision, link the successor from the old **Status** field and leave the rest intact.

| ADR | Decision |
|---|---|

## Template

```markdown
# NNNN — Decision title

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
What problem or constraint required a decision? Include material alternatives.

## Decision
What is adopted, and what is not?

## Consequences
What becomes easier, harder, or mandatory as a result?
```
